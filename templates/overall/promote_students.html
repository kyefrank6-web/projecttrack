O-level and A-level

  • O-level (S.1 → S.4) — S.1, S.2, and S.3 move up one class. S.4 leavers graduate (they do not become S.5).
  • A-level (S.5 → S.6) — S.5 is a new intake each year (upload students with new supervisors). Only existing S.5 learners move to S.6; S.6 leavers graduate.
  • Supervisor — unchanged for promoted learners (O-level S.1–S.3 and A-level S.5).
  • Scores & observations — all past assessments remain on each learner’s record.
  • UNEB cohort theme — S.3/S.4 learners keep the same cohort (e.g. S.3 cohort 2025 → S.4 in 2026 still uses cohort 2025).
